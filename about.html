About The Website

This website is made to direct any person interested in the PJO series to the right direction, whether in knowing the characters or seeing the main website of the saga. Thus, you need to know a few things:

  • Author Rick Riordan initially created the character of Percy Jackson to comfort his son, Haley, as his son suffered from ADHD and dyslexia, and in the saga most hero demigods have ADHD and dyselxia.

  • Some demigods, in the story, are born in a different way. Like how the children of Athena, where born from the thoughts of the goddess of wisdom and her intellect lover.

  • The five original books serve as the core for the whole universe of mythology that was built in it, being directly succeeded by the new five-book saga, The Heroes of Olympus.
